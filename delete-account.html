Fare-Ride Account Deletion

Fare-Ride allows riders and drivers to request permanent deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Enter your registered Fare-Ride email address.
  2. Click the red Delete My Account button.
  3. Confirm your deletion request.
  4. Send the prepared request using your email app.
  5. Fare-Ride support will verify your identity and process your request.

Request Account Deletion

Confirm Account Deletion Request

Warning: You are requesting permanent deletion of your Fare-Ride account and associated personal data. Certain records may be retained when required by applicable law.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

You may request deletion of:

  • Your Fare-Ride account
  • Your rider or driver profile
  • Personal contact information
  • Associated account data not required to be retained

Data Retention

Certain payment, transaction, safety, or legal records may need to be retained when required by applicable law.

Fare-Ride will explain any applicable retention periods when processing your request.

Delete Your Account Inside the App

You may also sign in to Fare-Ride and use the Delete Account option in your account settings.

Contact Fare-Ride

For assistance, use the support contact information provided in the Fare-Ride app.
