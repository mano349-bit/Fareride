Fare-Ride Account Deletion

Fare-Ride allows riders and drivers to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact Fare-Ride support through the contact method provided in the app.
  2. Use the subject "Delete My Fare-Ride Account".
  3. Include the email address registered with your Fare-Ride account.
  4. Support will verify your request and provide confirmation of deletion.

Data deletion

You may request deletion of your account, profile information, and associated personal data.

Certain transaction or legal records may need to be retained when required by law. Fare-Ride will explain any applicable retention periods.

Contact

Use the support contact information provided in the Fare-Ride app.
